Glass dome studies

Four ways to push the glass dome further. Every study also gets two fixes the first sketch lacked: electrodes drawn as small cups instead of flat dots, and labels on short leader lines pushed outward so they stop piling on each other. Drag to rotate, click an electrode or a channel to select it.

Channels

A1 · Real glass

Recommended
drag to rotate

The same dome made to behave like a piece of glass. It has thickness, so its edges catch more light. It reflects two soft studio lights, sits on a polished rim, and casts a soft shadow.

Good
Keeps everything you liked about A, but reads as a real object rather than a wireframe. The back electrodes still show through.
Watch
The reflections slide as you rotate, which is a little more for the Mac to draw.

A2 · Fitted cap

Most familiar to researchers
drag to rotate

The dome becomes a frosted EEG cap with a socket at every standard site. Your channels are cups seated in their sockets, and the empty sockets show where else an electrode could go.

Good
Anyone who has worn or fitted a cap recognises it at once. The empty sockets explain the montage without words.
Watch
Opaque, so the back of the head hides until you rotate. Less "glass" than the others.

A3 · Point field

Matches the intro
drag to rotate

The dome is drawn as a fine field of points with a soft glowing edge, and your channels are bright rings. The points echo the drifting particles in the intro.

Good
The lightest and most "scientific instrument" of the four. Nothing ever hides behind the surface.
Watch
The shape reads less solidly, especially in light mode.

A4 · Glass over a ghost head

Best orientation
drag to rotate

The glass dome sits over a faint outline of a head, drawn only as a glowing edge. Front, back and ears are obvious without a sculpted face.

Good
Fixes the dome's one weakness (which way is the front?) without the mannequin look you rejected.
Watch
Busier, and the outline is still a face, just a very faint one.

These can be combined

  • A1 + A2's sockets: real glass, with a faint socket ring at every unused standard site instead of grid lines.
  • A1 + A4's outline: real glass, with the ghost head switched on only in the Front, Left and Right views.
  • A3 for the intro, A1 for the Recording screen: the intro animates the point field and it settles into the glass dome when the step ends.
Electrode positions: the 10-10 system, Oostenveld & Praamstra (2001), the same table the app uses. Channels: the bundled PhysioNet EEGMMIDB S001 example. These are browser sketches of the look, not screenshots of the app; the chosen one is rebuilt natively.
